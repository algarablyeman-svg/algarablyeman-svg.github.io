*About Us:

We are the paramedics of the Palestine Red Crescent Society in the Gaza Strip, a team of young men and women who have dedicated our lives to saving lives.We are the first to arrive and the last to leave, working under bombardment and in the most difficult conditions. We carry a first-aid bag instead of a weapon, and our message is life.We make no distinction between anyone, we treat everyone, we transport the wounded, and we comfort the family of the martyr. We work 24 hours a day on number 101, because every second counts.We are the pride and hope of Gaza, and our resilience is the resilience of our people.
