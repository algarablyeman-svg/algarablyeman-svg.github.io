    1. Paramedic: Youssef Abu Nasser

    Youssef has been a volunteer with the Palestine Red Crescent Society for 5 years. He studied nursing at Al-Azhar University in Gaza. He is known among his colleagues for his speed and bravery in reaching the injured despite difficult conditions. He says: "My work is not just a job, it's a life-saving mission."

    2. Paramedic: Nour Al-Masri

    Nour is one of the first young female paramedics in the Strip. She studied emergency and ambulance services and volunteered to help women and children in particular. She works on the 101 ambulance service and is considered a role model for many girls who want to enter the medical field. Her message: "We want to stay by our people's side at every moment."
